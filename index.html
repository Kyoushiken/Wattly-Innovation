Wattly is a set of sensors that go inside your wall sockets. It shows you what each appliance is using right now, in an app on your phone.

Why it's hard to cut your power bill

Most people only see one number each month, so it's hard to know what is using the power.

Smart Plugs

They can't be used on big appliances, which are usually the ones using the most power.

Monthly Bills

They only show the total usage, not the breakdown of individual appliances.

Imported Monitors

Most are bought from overseas. A pack of 16 sensors on Amazon was about $250 NZD before shipping and installing.

How It Works

Wattly has three parts: the sensors, a hub and the app.

1. Sensors

Small sensors installed into your wall sockets that monitor the power each appliance uses.

2. Hub

A central device that collects data from the sensors and sends it to the app.

3. App

The app shows live use for each socket, sends alerts and gives suggestions on what to do next.

FAQ

A few things people might want to know.

Can I buy it yet?

No. Wattly is still a concept and we are building a waitlist for people who want to try it.

Who is it for?

Homeowners and renters who want to bring down their electricity bill. If you rent, check with your landlord before changing any fittings.

Do I have to pay every month?

No, the starter kit is a one-time purchase. The subscription for extra recommendations is optional.
